# Kotoba Lock Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

Kotoba Lock ("the app") shows a Japanese word, kanji, or phrase of the day on your Lock Screen and Home Screen, and lets you quiz yourself.

## Data we collect

**None.** Kotoba Lock does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts.** You don't sign in.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app has no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking.
- **On-device only.** Your review progress, quiz history, and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage and an App Group shared with the Kotoba Lock widget. Deleting the app deletes this data.
- **No network requests for content.** The word list is bundled inside the app.

## Purchases

Kotoba Lock Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status on your device with Apple's StoreKit.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
